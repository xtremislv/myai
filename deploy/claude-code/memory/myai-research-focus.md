The owner runs the YouTube channels TechWiser (@techwiser) and GadgetWiser (@wisergadget) and the TechWiser
Instagram, and uses research mainly for gadgets and products for videos. Good research = specific products with
prices, store links (Indian brand stores, `gadget_search`) and reference videos, not general prose.

Scouts: criteria are OR-style (pass at a number of met criteria), editable in the UI. TechWiser Instagram scout:
orderable/shippable to India (incl. import sites like Ubuy, Desertcart), budget cap 1 lakh INR, items over 50K need a
high score + several talking points + camera-friendly features; no video required for high scorers; learns rules from
review reasons; skips products our channels covered; ideas from competitor hits (@beebomco, @sillycornsmedia: >1M
views, >6 months old).

**How to apply:** favour product/store/YouTube sources; keep scout settings editable by the department.
