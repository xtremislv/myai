Never use the official department mailboxes (vault `mail/contact`, `mail/career`, `mail/finance`) for anything, not
even a login test or as a fallback sender, without asking the owner first. Alerts, new-link emails, reminders and
welcome emails go only from `mail/notify`, the owner's own alert account (Gmail app password in the vault).

**Why:** on 2026-11-05 contact@ was made the fallback sender without asking and sent mail; the owner said "don't ever
touch the official mails without asking".
**How to apply:** if `mail/notify` is missing or broken, say so and wait; never substitute another mailbox.
